ovayuvam

A private Android map that slowly reveals the places you have been.

Version 1 promise

  • Your revealed world stays local by default.
  • No ovayuva backend, account, ads, or social graph.
  • Real OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap tiles for the visible basemap.
  • Visited cells stay on the device.
  • The app asks for permission on launch and starts revealing immediately.
  • Tracking is visible through a persistent notification.
  • The main view is the full-screen fog map.
