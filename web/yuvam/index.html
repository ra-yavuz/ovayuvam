ovayuvam

A fog-of-war map for your own life. Walk, travel, come back later, and see the world you have uncovered.

Download APK Direct mirror Open source repo Roadmap

First version

On device

Your revealed world starts on your phone, not on an ovayuva server.

No account

No sign-in, public profile, friend graph, or backend is needed for version 1.

Real map

OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap tiles sit underneath the fog, styled in the ovayuva paper-and-ink direction.

Immediate reveal

The app asks for permission on launch and starts clearing the fog as you walk, using a soft circular trail instead of square chunks.
